## 2026-11-15: D2s runs: names outside a frame's shape, overflow area against shape transitions

The runs D2s's bar governs (docs/ledger/g3.md, "M1 decision bars", D2s:
on D2's winner). D2 is decided: `inline` slot frames
(`2026-11-14-d2-d3-decision.md`, the lead's decision). The D2S rows run on
robigo-rt since #667 (`assign` and `delayedAssign` bound,
`.Internal(delayedAssign)` ported), so this decision has its own base.

- Base: main `af7a7f19dc65c56b36c8952e823d2704164b2538` (#665 merged; it
  holds #667, #668, #670 and #672 too). Machine, valgrind and toolchain as
  in `2026-11-14-x-base-runs.md`.
- Variants, both from that base:
  - `overflow` (the default, N1b's): main itself,
    `~/.cache/x1-bins/robigo-rt-main-af7a7f19`.
  - `g3/x1-d2s-shapes` at `87272ace23f02c8faae9dd93f72746d1c8ac7767`,
    `~/.cache/x1-bins/robigo-rt-d2s-shapes-87272ace`. It adds
    `robigo-rtcore`'s `env/shapes.rs`:
    - when a closure frame gains a binding its shape has no slot for, the
      binding still goes to the frame's overflow block, and the shape is
      extended: a new shape (the old one's symbols and the name) linked
      from the old shape's ATTRIB word;
    - `new_closure_frame` makes every later frame from the newest
      extension, so the name has an inline slot there (unbound until
      defined, so listing and `exists` see what GNU R's frame holds) and no
      overflow block is made;
    - an extension's TRUELENGTH is how many of its symbols are the code's
      own (cmp.R's locals), and the promise compile environment
      (`Cenv::of_promise`) takes only those, so cmp.R's decisions do not
      change;
    - at most 8 names are added to a code's shape.

### What the rows exercise on robigo-rt

- `d2s-callee-assign` and `d2s-delayed` overflow on every call under
  `overflow`. `shapes` removes that block (one ENVSXP per call) after the
  first call.
- `d2s-assign` never overflows in either variant: cmp.R's `findLocals`
  takes a literal `assign("v", ...)` of two arguments as defining a local
  (`cmp.R`'s `findLocals1`), so `v` is in the frame's shape already.
- `d2s-eval` makes no overflow block in either variant: the frames
  allocated per call are the same with and without `v` assigned first by
  `<-`, and the eval'd `v <- 1` costs 3 LISTSXP per call instead, through
  a path this decision does not touch. Why `eval(quote(v <- 1))` binds
  without an overflow block is not settled here; it is left for P1. The
  binding itself lands in the closure's frame, as GNU R's does.
- `s3`: the method's frame overflows on every call under `overflow` (the
  dispatch's variables, `.Generic` and the rest, are not in a method's
  shape), and `shapes` removes those blocks: −10.6% instructions.

Where `shapes` spends (`d2s-callee-assign`, per iteration,
`scripts/callgrind-diff.py` at 10,000 against 5,000): `new_closure_frame`
+188 (following the shape's links and initialising the larger frames, on
every closure call of the row), `frame::bindings` +44 with `malloc`/`free`
+63 (`env_has_no_special_symbols` sorting a longer list), against
`alloc_slot_frame` −100 and `define_var` −77 for the block it saves. The
saving is at most what an overflow block costs a call, about 0.9% of
`d2s-callee-assign`; with two of the four rows never overflowing, the
D2S geomean could not fall by more than about 0.5% even with no overhead.
