## 2026-11-15: D9 decision: clear at last use, by the tie band

- Bar: docs/ledger/g3.md, "M1 decision bars", D9. A variant whose
  finalizer does not run at the same `gc()` call as in GNU R 4.6.2 is out;
  of the rest, the lower geomean instructions over `fib` and CALLS wins,
  and inside the tie band (±1%) `lastuse`.
- Runs: `2026-11-15-d9-runs.md` (`g3/v1-d9-liveness` at `4fbe3c6e`,
  against main `af7a7f19`).
- Result:
  - Exactness: both variants deliver every dead-root finalizer at GNU R's
    `gc()`, in both profiles (`tests/finalizers.rs`, 7/7 each). Both stay.
  - Instructions: liveness ÷ lastuse = 0.9975 over `fib` and CALLS, 0.25%
    lower, inside the tie band.
- **Verdict: `lastuse`, the default on main.** Nothing merges.
- Notes:
  - The variant's cost and saving are both small and of the same order:
    it drops the clear tests and ops from the VM loop (37-59 instructions
    a call) and adds an activation link and two registers per call (44).
  - Not every clear is for the collector (reading `links.rs`, not a test
    here): a complex assignment's right-hand side is cleared so that
    `links::commit` gives its value one link, not two (bcEval's stack
    holds only live values), so the variant kept those clears and masks
    before `commit`. Any later change to clearing has to keep that.
