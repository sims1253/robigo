## 2026-11-15: P1 step 1, frames: no list per call for the special-symbol check, slot-indexed locals

The first P1 family (base and bars: `2026-11-15-p1-base.md`): costs a
closure call pays in its frame that GNU R does not.

- Base: main `a8bf641a`. Change: branch `g3/p1-frames` at
  `072da23639d9847a5418cd10ec0028b171223a11`, `robigo-rtcore` and
  `robigo-rt`:
  - `Cx::env_has_no_special_symbols` (GNU R's `R_envHasNoSpecialSymbols`,
    asked by `applyClosure` on every call) built and sorted a `Vec` of the
    frame's bindings by listing order to ask whether any binding is a
    special symbol; the answer does not depend on the order, so it now
    walks the bound slots in place (`frame::walk_bound`), allocating
    nothing;
  - a closure's reads (`GetVar`) and unboxed stores (`SetVar`) of its own
    formals and locals carry their slot index: `code::build` writes it
    into the instruction's free `f` field from the closure's shape, and
    the VM goes to that slot directly (`Cx::frame_slot_raw`,
    `Cx::frame_store_scalar_raw`) when the frame is of the code's shape
    (one compare) and the slot holds a value, an unboxed scalar or an
    argument, or (for a store) takes one as `define_scalar`'s fast path
    does; anything else (another frame, an unbound, active, locked or
    cell-backed slot) takes the full lookup or `defineVar` as before.
