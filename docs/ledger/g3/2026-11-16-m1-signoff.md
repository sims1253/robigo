### To settle before M2/M3 planning

1. **The baseline gap.** The oracle image's GNU R counts 3.9% to 12.9%
   more instructions than the local build. Nobody has established why;
   likely causes are glibc 2.41's CPU dispatch and Debian's build flags.
   E1's s3 verdict depends on it (0.991x in `perf-rt`, 1.014x against the
   local build). No owner yet.
2. **`perf-rt`'s CPU and baseline policy:**
   - print the runner's CPU;
   - re-measure GNU R on CI's runner class at each milestone, not on one
     developer machine.

   The cycles study adds that instructions differ by up to 0.5% between
   machines even for one binary. No owner yet.
3. **The eager-evaluation proof for fib:** that evaluating a nested call's
   leaf arguments early is unobservable (`p1-fib-entry.md`). It is the way
   to E2's bar other than G8's native tier. It needs an owner, or a
   decision to decline it.
4. **Estimated cycles in `perf-rt`: gate, report-only, or nothing.**
   - **The question:** the council's first round split. Gemini wanted
     them gated, Opus report-only, and Sol wanted separate milestone runs.
   - **The study** (`2026-11-16-cycles-study.md`, the lead's decision
     (c)) recommends keeping the instruction gate and reporting
     estimated cycles under a pinned cache model, with no failure. It
     also recommends measuring wall time itself on fixed hardware at
     milestones.
   - **What is open:** the lead's decision on the study.
