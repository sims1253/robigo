- Where the instructions went. Neither of the row's closures ran inline:
  `check`, `if (is.null(t[[1]])) 1 else 1 + check(t[[1]]) +
  check(t[[2]])`, uses `[[` (byte code's `StartSubset` and `DfltSubset`)
  and `is.null` (`CallBuiltin`), which the verdict did not take. Each
  iteration made 1,022 real calls of about 6,100 instructions, 511 of them
  `check`'s.
- What changes (`crates/robigo-rt/src/inline.rs`):
  - **The verdict** admits `x[[i]]` with one unnamed index and `is.null`
    besides `invisible`.
  - **`x[[i]]`.** `StartSubset` gives up on an object, which would
    dispatch on `[[`. `DfltSubset` reads a plain list: a VECSXP, not
    ALTREP, at an index `vm::num` takes (an unboxed number, or a plain
    length-1 vector: the compiler loads `[[`'s constant index boxed), a
    double of at least 1 truncated as `get1index` truncates, or an integer,
    in range. The element is `VECTOR_ELT(x, k)`, as `do_subset2_dflt`
    returns it, with no count changed. Anything else gives up, and the
    real call raises its errors and warnings.
  - **`is.null`** gives the logical `do_is` builds into the register, with
    `R_Visible` on, after the safepoint check `invisible` has.
  - **Argument code.** The recursive calls' `t[[1]]` is forced inline too:
    `pure` gains the base guard (`Scope::base_guard`: the real caller's
    lookup, or for an inlined frame a give-up when it binds the name), its
    jump, and the two subset instructions. As for its other instructions,
    `pure` leaves `R_Visible` to the reading `GetVar`.
  - The fused-step decoder counts `StartSubset`'s jump target as one; an
    inline run never takes it.
- What stays real: the outermost `check(make(8))`, whose argument is a
  call `pure` does not run, and `make`'s 511 calls, which build lists.
  About 8 `check` calls per iteration give up besides; most likely the
  evaluation count's poll past 1000 and due collections, which the real
  calls take.
