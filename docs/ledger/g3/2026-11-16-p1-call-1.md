- **Step 1, no allocation at a call's end** (`b3a6ed30`, `robigo-rtcore`).
  `end_call` took the frame's entries from the residue list by
  partitioning the whole list into two new vectors whenever the list held
  any entry, also another frame's. A generic's frame keeps its ghost while
  its method runs, so every method call's end paid two allocations. Now a
  frame with no entries returns at once. A frame's entries are extracted
  in place and handed to the cleanup one by one, in their order, and the
  others keep theirs.
- **Step 2, the matcher's buffer** (`bce786da`, `robigo-rt`). `args`'s
  small vector (`Buf`) asked on every push and read whether its heap
  vector was empty, and `push` was a call: about 200 instructions of a
  plain call. Now the length says where the elements are: inline up to
  8, all on the heap past that. The push and the reads are inline, and
  the one spill is a cold function.
