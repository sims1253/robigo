## 2026-11-18: A3, robigo-rt's PGO build and §13's wall-time harness

- **Why:** docs/g3.md §13 compares wall time on the shipped (PGO) build. The
  plan's A3 (the lead's decision (a): §13 in full from M3) asks for
  `pgo-build.sh -p robigo-rtapi` and `wall13.py` in `scripts/`, run on the
  5900X and on m0pad.
- **PGO:** `scripts/pgo-build.sh -p robigo-rtapi profile` trains an
  instrumented robigo-rt on:
  - every row of `benchmarks/rt/calls-rows.toml`;
  - E1's three programs;
  - the boot, five times;
  - the rt conformance run.

  Then `use FILE` builds with the profile. Training details:
  - The instrumented build gets `-vp-counters-per-site=8`. With LLVM's
    default of four, the evaluator's indirect calls run out of counters,
    and LLVM then says so on stderr in every process: 358 rt cases failed
    their stderr comparison for that alone.
  - 10 rows stop with an error under robigo-rt, on the plain build as well:
    `d0-cbind`, `d0-rbind`, `source5k`, and #829's seven `jit-*` rows. They
    train what they reach.
  - The conformance run passes under the instrumented binary.
- **Instructions, PGO against the plain build of the same commit** (eec051b83
  plus this change; perf.py rt-bars): every row gets cheaper.
  - plain-call rows: −2.7% to −6.8%;
  - fib: −11.3%; binarytrees: −11.9%;
  - e7-paste −10.9%, e7-match −51.5%, e7-unique −42.4%;
  - guard and d0 rows: −9% to −25%;
  - peak RSS: within 1%.

  The rows are the training set, so these are in-sample numbers.
- **`scripts/wall13.py`:** the M1 exit's one-program script
  (`data/2026-11-16-m1-exit-c536597d/wall13.py`), generalised:
  - any number of programs (`--filter`), and rivals from `--impls`;
  - robigo-rt run on the host with no `R_HOME`, because rivals.py's Runner
    sets GNU R's, which robigo-rt now ignores with a warning;
  - it records the machine (CPU model and signature), robigo-rt's binary
    identity, and the load average at the start and end;
  - the report warns when the 1-minute load passed a quarter of the CPUs.
- **First runs, on the 5900X under load.** The load average was 18–30 on 24
  CPUs, from other agents' builds: these show the harness works, not §13's
  numbers.
  - The final run, every runtime on base alone, PGO robigo-rt against GNU R
    4.6.2: faster on all three E1 programs, at 0.441x on binarytrees, 0.965x
    on calls and 0.260x on fib.
  - Two earlier runs let GNU R attach utils and stats inside the clock (Sol's
    round 1 found it; now fixed). GNU R's medians there were 0.28 s against
    0.17 s for binarytrees.
  - In those runs the plain build was at 0.454x, 1.032x (slower) and
    0.221x, and GNU R's own medians moved by up to 35% between runs.
- **m0pad** was just as busy: a load of 20–24 on 12 CPUs. Its disk is 98%
  full (22 GB free), and robigo-rt has to be built there, because it finds
  its R_HOME tree through the checkout path compiled into it. Not run.
