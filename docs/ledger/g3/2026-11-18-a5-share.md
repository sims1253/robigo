## 2026-11-18: A5, R_HOME's share/, as.environment(<list>), and the A5 bar probe

- Why: A5's bar is `loadNamespace("tools")` and `tools:::makeLazyLoading`
  under robigo-rt. A probe with stand-ins for the other tracks' missing
  pieces (kept outside the repository, `~/.cache/robigo-research/a1/a5-probe/`,
  rerun with `bar.sh <worktree>`) ran both. It found two gaps of A1's,
  fixed here:
  - tools reads `share/encodings/Adobe-glyphlist` and
    `share/licenses/license.db` while it loads, and the tree had no `share/`;
  - `as.environment(<list>)`.
- **share/:** `scripts/rt-home.sh` installs the tarball's `share/` as
  share/Makefile.in's `install-sources` does, each glob in its order. With
  4.6.2's tarball, the tree's share/ is byte-identical with the oracle
  image's installed one, except for:
  - the five `.rds` files of `install-sources2` (`bibliographies/R.rds`,
    `dictionaries/*.rds`), which GNU R's build makes with R and utils (L4's
    build);
  - no `zoneinfo`: the oracle's configure uses the system's time zones.

  A glob that matches nothing stops the script, so a new tarball can't lose
  files without anyone noticing.
- **as.environment(<list>):** envir.c's VECSXP case. It calls
  `list2env(x, NULL, emptyenv())` evaluated in the caller's environment, so
  a `list2env` defined there is the one called, as in GNU R. Case:
  tests/rt/env/as-environment-list.R.
- **tools' natives, CI-checked without stand-ins:**
  tests/rt/dynload/tools-natives.R calls 17 of tools' routines through
  their registered symbols (dyn.load, no tools R code). The md5 and sha256
  digests are taken of R_HOME's share/ files. Recorded on 4.6.2, and the
  output is identical.
- **Conformance runner:** `robigo_conformance::Options::env` holds changes
  to each case's environment. robigo-rt's conformance test passes
  `RT_CAPI_LIBS` and drops `R_HOME` through it, instead of
  `std::env::set_var` / `remove_var` on its own process (#838's deferred
  low; that code relied on the test having only one thread).
- **The probe's findings for the other tracks**, routed by the foreman:
  - **L:** readDCF, readLines, scan, textConnection, the
    `options(keep.source=)` refusal, and `.AutoloadEnv` (INTERIM(L5));
  - **S1:** matrix `[<-` and subsetting by dimnames;
  - **B:** cbind, rbind, t.default, split, make.names, make.unique,
    tolower, chartr, Sys.time, Sys.setenv, Sys.unsetenv, iconv, format,
    dir.create, tempfile, tempdir;
  - **the trial delegate:** seq.int;
  - **X:** Recall;
  - **C1:** `_R_COMPILE_PKGS_=1` writes uncompiled databases.
    serialize.c:1040's `R_cmpfun1` on write is missing, and serialize does
    not refuse.
