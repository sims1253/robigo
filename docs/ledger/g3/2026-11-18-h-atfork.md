## 2026-11-18: one set of fork handlers for the process's locks

- Why: two open findings.
  - #851's review: the symbol arena registered its `pthread_atfork`
    handlers lazily, inside its first lock (`Once::call_once`). A fork
    from one thread while another was inside that first `call_once` left
    the child's `Once` in progress for ever.
  - H's note on #842: B's `LIVE_ISOLATES` count and clib's `ENV` lock had
    no fork handlers at all. A child forked while another thread held one
    blocked on its first isolate start or end, or its first `Sys.setenv`.
- What:
  - `robigo-heap/src/fork.rs`: one `pthread_atfork` triple for the
    process, registered eagerly by the first `Core::new`.
    - `prepare` takes the embedder's locks first, then the arena's. The
      embedder's are given by `set_fork_locks`, and robigo-rtcore gives
      `LIVE_ISOLATES` and then `ENV` exclusive.
    - `parent` and `child` release them in the reverse order, only what
      that `prepare` took.
    - The module's doc holds the process's lock order, the one place it is
      written: `LIVE_ISOLATES`, `ENV`, std's environment lock (taken only
      under `ENV`), the arena.
    - It also says what `prepare` waits for: a reader holding `ENV` shared
      for a C call finishes it, and a thread blocked on a lock stays
      blocked in the parent until `parent` releases it and does not exist
      in the child. No robigo code forks while it holds one of these locks.
  - `arena.rs` loses its lazy registration and keeps `lock_for_fork` and
    `unlock_after_fork` for the triple.
  - `isolate.rs`: `fork_take` and `fork_release` hold the two guards in a
    static between the handlers; only the forking thread touches it.
  - `clib.rs`: `env_write_lock`, for the handlers. B's latest push made
    `env_locale` and `c_locale` take `ENV` shared, so `prepare` must take
    it exclusive, and does.
  - `docs/g3.md`: a note under L6. `ENV` and `LIVE_ISOLATES` are
    process-global by necessity (one environment per process), hold no R
    state, and are ordered in `fork.rs`.
  - Two stale docs: `Sexp::sym_id` (ids are the process's, in the arena's
    order) and `shared.rs`'s `symbol` (the arena's `new_symbol`).
- Tests (`robigo-rtcore/src/fork_tests.rs`, not under Miri):
  - One per lock: `LIVE_ISOLATES`, `ENV` exclusive, `ENV` shared, the
    arena. Another thread holds the lock for 200 ms while this one forks.
  - The child starts an isolate, writes the environment through the
    `Sys.setenv` writer, interns a new symbol, and must exit 0 within 20 s.
  - All four pass with the handlers. With registration disabled, all four
    time out: the child never finishes.
  - The heap's own fork test now says what it covers (heap-only users) and
    what it does not (the interleaving is the scheduler's).

  against B's branch therefore shows main's commits too until #842 is
  retargeted.
