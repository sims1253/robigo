## 2026-11-21: the rt job in three shards

- **Why:** the one `rt` job ran every step of scripts/rt-ci.py in 29-38
  minutes against a 40-minute limit. #900, on the critical path, was
  cancelled at 40 minutes inside the last step. #907 raised the limit to 60
  as a stopgap; this is the fix.
- **The steps' times** (main's 2f16dc4c, run 44607483451, the rt-ci.py
  step 35.3 min):

  | step | min |
  |---|---:|
  | release tests (8.4 of it the release build, the tests about 20 s) | 8.75 |
  | conformance under ROBIGO_GC_MOVE=young:16 with gctorture | 10.00 |
  | conformance under ROBIGO_RETRY_TORTURE=1 (its feature rebuild and run) | 6.66 |
  | conformance under gctorture | 4.15 |
  | builtin differential (23 rows; about 5 with #887's 87 rows and plant build) | 1.70 |
  | ROBIGO_GC_MOVE unit tests with gctorture | 0.79 |
  | the other 15 steps | 3.7 |

- **The split** (the foreman's choice, option A: three shards, no runner
  change):
  - `rt` (modes): the release build and tests, the conformance and its
    observation modes, gctorture, quarantine, pin report, stress,
    overshoot, E9, the byte-code crosscheck: about 16 min, then L1's rdb
    round trip. Timeout 35.
  - `rt-gc-move`: the moving collector's unit tests and conformance:
    about 19 min (8.4 build, 10 conformance). Timeout 40.
  - `rt-feature-builds`: the builtin differential with its plants, and
    retry-torture: about 15 min now, about 20 after #887. Timeout 40.

  The wall time goes from 35 to about 19 minutes, for about +17
  runner-minutes per run (two more builds).
- **Not chosen:** five shards, with the GC_MOVE conformance itself halved,
  would give about 15 minutes. That needs a `ROBIGO_CONF_SHARD` in the
  conformance runner and about +34 runner-minutes per run. 19 minutes is
  already under the other long jobs, so it would not shorten a PR's cycle.
- **What a shared build would save** (recorded, not built): one job builds
  the release test binaries once and the shards download them. That would
  save the two extra 8.4-minute builds, about 17 runner-minutes per run,
  and about 8 minutes of each shard's wall time, so `rt-gc-move` would take
  about 11 minutes. The cost: an artifact of the test binaries and
  robigo-rt (`target/release/deps` of the rt crates, several GB with debug
  info), which has to upload and download inside the budget. The 134 MB
  `builtin-diff-data` artifact stalled once on upload (#887).
- **Mechanics:**
  - `rt-ci.py --shard NAME` runs one shard's steps, in rt-ci.py's order,
    and without `--shard` a local run is unchanged.
  - Each step's shard comes from one rule, `shard_of`. The R_HOME tree (3
    s) is made in every shard; every other step is in exactly one
    (scripts/test-rt-ci.py).
  - Every shard prints its steps' wall times, slowest first, when it ends,
    whether it passed or not.
  - Only `rt` saves the rust cache and the R tarball cache, so two jobs
    never race to save one key.
  - ci-check.py lets the two new jobs be skipped only as `rt` is (rt mode
    false), and `Check` needs them.
