# H: PRINTNAME encoding marks, and the #894 follow-ups (2026-11-21)

- **PRINTNAME marks** (A1's #887 low).
  - The problem: GNU R's `installTrChar` calls `installNoTrChar` when no translation is needed, and a new symbol's PRINTNAME is then the CHARSXP itself, mark and all, when it is ASCII or UTF-8 in a UTF-8 locale. robigo always installed native bytes, so `ls()` after `.Internal(assign(<UTF-8 name>, ...))`, and serialization, showed "unknown" where GNU R shows "UTF-8".
  - The fix: `Cx::install_tr(c, name)` keeps `c` through `Core::install_char`, after an ownership check, with a fallback for shared CHARSXPs. B's `text::install_tr_char` (#919), `coerce_to_symbol` (`as.name`), the serialize reader, `Rf_installTrChar` and subassign's tags use it.
  - The reader, `Rf_installTrChar` and subassign's tags now also give `install_tr_name`'s warning and errors (Sol S1–S3).
  - Cases:
    - `tests/rt/env/symbol-encoding.R` (UTF-8): marks in `ls()`, `as.list()` and serialization, and a new symbol round-tripped through `unserialize`;
    - `symbol-encoding-c.R` (C locale): a "bytes" tag is refused, and a UTF-8 symbol unserialized there warns and gets the escaped name.
  - Not changed, for their owners: `compile.rs:2628` and `specials.rs:1227` (C1), and `lower.rs:59`, the parser's string tags (S1).
